        Where the numbers come from. Generic whole foods use per-100 g values from standard nutrition tables and are reliable. Chain items are stored per serving from each chain's published US figures — regional menus differ, sometimes a lot, so treat them as close rather than exact. Anything marked (est.), including the local dishes, is an informed estimate with no official source: Al Baik and most regional restaurants publish no nutrition data. Those can be 30–40% out. If you have the packet, trust the packet and save your own version.
